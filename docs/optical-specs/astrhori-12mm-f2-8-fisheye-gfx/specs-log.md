## Caveats

- Full-frame fisheye design mounted on GFX — produces circular fisheye image (does not cover 44x33mm sensor)
- Same optical design across all mounts (E, Z, R, L, GFX)
- MTF shows excellent performance: 10 lp/mm ~0.95–1.0 across entire field; 30 lp/mm ~0.85 center, ~0.7 edge
- "nano-multi-layer coating, front lens is waterproof" — first AstrHori lens with a named coating
