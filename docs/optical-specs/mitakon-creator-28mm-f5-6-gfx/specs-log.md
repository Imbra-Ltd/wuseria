## Optical construction

- **Elements / Groups:** 8 / 7 (zyoptics.net spec table, confirmed by FujiRumors/dc.watch press release)
- **Special elements:** 1 ED, 3 ultra-high refractive index, 2 ultra-high transmission — from ZY Optics press release via FujiRumors: "8 elements in 7 groups (1 ED, 3 ultra-high refractive index, 2 ultra-high transmission)"
- **Coating:** not published
- **Construction diagram:** found — two versions saved. `construction-diagram.jpg` (from eBay, Chinese labels). `construction-diagram-official.jpg` (from DPReview forum, official ZY Optics branding with English labels: Extra-low Dispersion, Low Dispersion, High Refraction Index elements). Element count in diagram appears higher than 8 — may include elements from multiple cemented groups counted individually; verify against official 8E/7G spec.
- **MTF chart:** found — saved as `mtf-chart.jpg` (from eBay/Shoten Kobo listing, official ZY Optics material). Title "28 5.6". Shows 10 and 30 lp/mm S/T at image heights 0–21.5mm (full-frame coverage). Strong center performance (~0.95 at 10 lp/mm), edge drops to ~0.82/0.88.
