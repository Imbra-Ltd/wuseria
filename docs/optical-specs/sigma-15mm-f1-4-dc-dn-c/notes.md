## Astigmatism assessment

S/M divergence at 30 lp/mm:

- Center to ~10mm: S and M very tight, gap < 0.03
- 12.5-14mm: slight separation, gap ~0.04
- 10 lp/mm lines nearly overlapping throughout

**Scoring:** Near-overlap with slight divergence at edge → **1.5**

Note: No lab astigmatism data exists for this lens. MTF chart used as fallback per ADR-014.
