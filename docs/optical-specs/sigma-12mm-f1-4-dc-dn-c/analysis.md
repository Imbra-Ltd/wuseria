- Solid = Sagittal (S), Dashed = Meridional (M)

## Astigmatism assessment

S/M divergence at 30 lp/mm:

- Center to ~10mm: S and M track closely, gap < 0.03
- 10-14mm edge: modest divergence, gap ~0.05-0.08
- 10 lp/mm lines nearly overlapping throughout

**Scoring:** Near-overlap with slight divergence at extreme edge → **1.5**
