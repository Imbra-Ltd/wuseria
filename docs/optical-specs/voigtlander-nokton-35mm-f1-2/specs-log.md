## Classification

- Character tier: Tier 1 — purpose-built character lens
- Design family: Traditional "8-element" style, all spherical, 6 groups / 8 elements

## Caveats

- Voigtlander deliberately withholds MTF from Noktons; APO-LANTHARs get MTF. Confirmed corporate policy.
- All-spherical design (no aspherical elements) = classic rendering with intentional aberrations wide open.
- Catalog describes it as delivering "smooth and large bokeh" (なめらかで大きなボケ味).
