## Caveats

- Product discontinued from Lensbaby site; replaced by Edge 35 (f/3.5, 9e/6g — different optic)
- Sweet optic creates a spot of focus surrounded by blur (different from Edge's slice of focus)
- The Composer Pro II body tilts 0-15°, adding selective focus control
- officialUrl in lenses.ts points to collection page since product page is gone
- Same optical design across all mounts
