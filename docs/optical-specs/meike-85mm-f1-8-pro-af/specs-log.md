## Findings

- **opticalElements:** 11 (LensTip, OpticalLimits)
- **opticalGroups:** 8 (LensTip, OpticalLimits)
- **specialElements:** 2 ED, 1 aspherical, 3 HR (LensTip: "2 ED glass elements", "1 aspherical element", "3 HR glass elements"); official GB page says 2 ED + 3 HR + 1 UHR (no aspherical). LensTip's aspherical retained as it's a shape, not a glass type — both could be correct.
- **coating:** double-sided multi-layer (official GB E-mount page: "double-sided Multilayer coating")
- **filterThread:** 62mm (LensTip; DB has 58mm — discrepancy)
- **maxMagnification:** 0.11 (already in DB; LensTip says 0.13x, OpticalLimits says 0.11x)
- **constructionDiagram:** not found
- **MTF chart:** found on OpticalLimits (Sony FE mount review)

## Caveats

- LensTip does not list Fujifilm X as available mount — but official Meike page confirms X-mount
- Same optical design across all mounts — OpticalLimits Sony FE review is relevant
- HR glass (high-refractive index glass) tracked in specialElements alongside ED
- OpticalLimits MTF chart URL: opticallimits.com/images/8Reviews/lenses/meike_85_18pro/mtf.png
