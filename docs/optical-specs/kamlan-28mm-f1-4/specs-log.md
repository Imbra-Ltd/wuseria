## Findings

- **opticalElements:** 8 (official page, confirmed by construction diagram)
- **opticalGroups:** 7 (official page, confirmed by construction diagram)
- **specialElements:** 6 HR (Adorama, CeX, lens-db, Photography Life "6 HRI elements", construction diagram)
- **coating:** multi-coating (Alik Griffin review, no proprietary name)
- **maxMagnification:** 0.15x (Photography Life spec table)
- **afMotor:** manual focus only (official page: "對焦方式：手動")

## Caveats

- Photography Life lists weight 384g and dimensions 55x65mm — these are non-X-mount values (PL lists Canon EF-M, MFT, Sony E mounts only). DB uses official Kamlan X-mount values (348g, 66x58mm).
- Construction diagram from allphotolenses.com saved as `kamlan-28mm-f1-4-construction.png`
