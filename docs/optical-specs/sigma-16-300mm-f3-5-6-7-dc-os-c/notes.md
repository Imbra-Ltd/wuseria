## Astigmatism assessment

S/M divergence at 30 lp/mm:

- Center: gap already ~0.06
- 5mm: massive divergence begins, S at 0.83, M drops to 0.66. Gap ~0.17
- Divergence persists across the entire frame
- 10 lp/mm lines also show significant separation from 5mm onward

**Scoring:** Heavy divergence starting early across entire frame → **0.5**
