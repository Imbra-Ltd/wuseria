## Caveats

- **Different optical designs across mounts:** The X-mount CINE version (7 elements / 10 groups) has a completely different optical formula from the Leica M-mount version (12 elements / 7 groups with 7 Tantalum elements). The M-mount version is a larger, heavier lens (945-968g vs 665g).
- The "7 elements in 10 groups" figure is unusual (elements < groups is atypical in lens design). This may be a transposition error in SLR Magic's marketing, but it is consistent across their official spec sheet, Fuji Rumors press release, Fuji Addict, and B&H listing.
- The M-mount version explicitly includes 7 Tantalum (high refractive index) glass elements. Whether the X-mount redesign retains Tantalum elements is not disclosed.
- SLR Magic has never published an optical construction diagram for any version of this lens.
