## Astigmatism assessment

S/M divergence at 30 lp/mm:

- Center to ~10mm: S and M track together, gap < 0.05
- 12.5-14mm: significant divergence, S ~0.57, M drops to ~0.40. Gap ~0.17

**Scoring:** Heavy divergence at extreme edge → **1.0**

Note: LensTip lab review already provides astigmatism: 1.0 (from measured data). Lab data takes precedence.
