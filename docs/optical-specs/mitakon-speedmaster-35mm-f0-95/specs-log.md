## Optical construction

- **Elements / Groups:** 10 / 7 (LensTip spec table)
- **Special elements:** none listed by LensTip
- **Coating:** "AMC" per model name on AllPhotoLenses (likely Anti-reflection Multi-Coating), but LensTip transmission review measured only 85-86% max (~1.1% loss per air-to-glass surface across 14 surfaces) — "very average result, not much better than good old magnesium fluoride coatings". Marketing name may overstate actual coating quality.
- **Construction diagram:** not found
- **MTF chart:** not found (LensTip review has resolution charts but not manufacturer MTF)

## Caveats

- Discontinued in 2016, replaced by Mk II (11E/8G, different optical design)
- Official zyoptics.net product page now shows Mk II data; no separate Mk I page exists
- LensTip review (July 2015) is the only lab review of this lens
