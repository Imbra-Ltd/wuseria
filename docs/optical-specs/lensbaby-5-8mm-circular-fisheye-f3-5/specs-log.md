## Caveats

- Product discontinued from Lensbaby site
- 185° circular fisheye — produces a circular image within the frame
- No filter thread (bulbous front element)
- Focal length is 5.8mm (corrected from rounded 6mm in lenses.ts)
- B&H lists "12mm" which is the full-frame equivalent
- No optical construction data found from any source — even ePhotozine (trust-3) lists "No Data"
- Weight corrected from 190g to 298g (ePhotozine, m43photo) — 190g was likely optic-only weight
- All-plastic construction with polished inner barrel (intentional flare effect)
- Image circle 14.6mm — optimized for APS-C, incomplete circle on MFT
