# XC 16-50mm f/3.5-5.6 OIS — Optical Specs Notes

- Official Fujifilm specifications page shows wrong MTF charts (XF 70-300mm data)
- MTF chart source: Photography Life
  https://photographylife.com/lenses/fujifilm-xc-16-50mm-f3-5-5-6-ois-ii
- Charts are combined wide+tele images, not split by frequency
- Construction diagram sourced from Digital Photography Live (official Fujifilm
  diagram rehosted): https://digitalphotographylive.com/fujinon-xc-16-50mm-f3-5-5-6-ois-ii-lens/
- Diagram labels 3 aspherical elements; 1 ED element not color-coded (confirmed
  via specs text: 12 elements in 10 groups, 3 aspherical, 1 ED)
