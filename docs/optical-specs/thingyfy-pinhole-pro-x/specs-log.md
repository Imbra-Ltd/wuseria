## Notes

This is a pinhole lens — light passes through a laser-drilled hole, not through glass elements. Conventional optical specs (elements, groups, coatings, MTF) do not apply. The lens has no optical quality to measure in the traditional sense; image quality is determined by pinhole diameter and sensor distance.
