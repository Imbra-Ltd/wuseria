## Classification

- Character tier: Tier 1 — purpose-built character lens
- Design family: Double Gauss derivative, 6 groups / 10 elements, 1 aspherical, 2 ED

## Caveats

- Voigtlander deliberately withholds MTF charts from all Nokton lenses; APO-LANTHAR lenses get MTF (SVG on website + catalog). Confirmed corporate policy.
- The X-mount version (6g/10e with aspherical + ED) is a different design from the VM mount version.
