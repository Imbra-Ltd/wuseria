## Optical construction

- 16 elements in 11 groups (2 aspherical, 3 ED)
- Coating: Frog Eye Coating (hydrophobic front element)

## Caveats

- Venus Laowa does not name their anti-reflective multi-coating system; lenses without Frog Eye Coating are listed as generic multi-coating
- Frog Eye Coating is a hydrophobic front-element coating (like fluorine), not an anti-reflective optical coating
