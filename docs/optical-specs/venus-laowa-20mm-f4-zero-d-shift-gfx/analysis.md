## Chart legend

- Y-axis: MTF contrast (0–1)
- X-axis: image height (mm) — 0–21 mm unshifted (GFX sensor coverage),
  0–32 mm shifted (extended image circle for full shift range)
- Curves: 10S, 10T, 30S, 30T (10 lp/mm and 30 lp/mm sagittal + tangential)
- Aperture: f/4 in both panels
- Shift lens: panels show the lens's MTF across the unshifted sensor
  coverage and across the larger image circle the lens covers when
  shifted
