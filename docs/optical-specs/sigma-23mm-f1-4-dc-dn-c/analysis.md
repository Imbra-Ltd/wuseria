## Chart legend

- At 23mm focal length
- Solid = Sagittal (S), Dashed = Meridional (M)
- Red lines = 10 lp/mm (contrast), Blue lines = 30 lp/mm (resolution)
- X-axis: image height (mm), Y-axis: contrast (0–1)

## Astigmatism assessment

S/M divergence at 30 lp/mm:

- Center to ~10mm: S and M nearly overlapping, gap < 0.03
- 12.5mm: divergence begins, gap ~0.05
- 14mm: heavy divergence, S at ~0.70, M drops to ~0.45. Gap ~0.25

**Scoring:** Heavy divergence at extreme edge → **1.0**

Note: LensTip lab review already provides astigmatism: 2.0 (from measured data). Lab data takes precedence.
