## Astigmatism assessment

S/M divergence at 30 lp/mm:

FF lens. 10 lp/mm: M rises above S at edge (crossing). 30 lp/mm: both drop with moderate gap. At f/8: heavy divergence at FF edge. LensTip lab measured 10.4% (1.0).
