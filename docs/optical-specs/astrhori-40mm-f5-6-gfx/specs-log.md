## Caveats

- Official text lists "two high-refractive glass" and "2 ED lenses" as separate items
- 4 special elements out of 7 total — high ratio but cinegear.nl explicitly confirms
- Product Parameters says "APS-C" format — this is incorrect, it is a GFX medium format lens
- MTF at 10 lp/mm: ~0.95 center, ~0.85 at 27.5mm; at 40 lp/mm: ~0.8 center, drops sharply to ~0.2 at edge
