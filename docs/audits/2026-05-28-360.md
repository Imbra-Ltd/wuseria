## 1. Value (user perspective) — A-

**Method:** evaluated only user-facing materials (README "what it does",
built `dist/` HTML, nav, meta) against the 360 Value sub-dimensions, with no
access to developer docs.

### Promises extracted

1. Scores every Fuji X/GFX lens by genre ("Every Fujifilm lens. Scored for
   your genre." / "243 lenses rated against 9 photography genres").
2. Optical scores from trusted lab reviews, not marketing specs.
3. Lens Explorer — browse/filter/sort by specs.
4. Camera Explorer — sortable body specs.
5. Accessories with compatibility.
6. Genre Guide — per-genre screeners (EV matrices, FL chips, scored rankings),
   9 genres.
7. Wiki — terms/concepts with category filter + search.
8. Per-lens/camera/accessory detail pages.
9. Workflow journeys ("Find your gear", "Plan your shoot").
10. Lightweight/field-ready ("works on 3G").
11. Free, independent, author-identified, feedback path.

### Findings

| #   | Sub-dimension               | Status  | Finding                                                                                                                                                         |
| --- | --------------------------- | ------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Functional completeness     | PASS    | All 5 nav items resolve to real, populated sections. 461 pages built (243 lens / 39 camera / 47 accessory / 115 wiki / 9 genre). Homepage stats match data.     |
| 2   | Functional completeness     | PASS    | Tables server-rendered (full dataset in island props), so browse/sort/filter work; filters present; no pagination dead-ends, no empty tables.                   |
| 3   | Functional completeness     | PASS    | 563 internal links checked across key pages — zero broken. Detail pages cross-link to alternatives. 404 page with recovery link exists.                         |
| 4   | Functional completeness     | PASS    | Genre Guide (flagship) fully delivers: EV/scene selector, equipment list, interactive EV matrix (Rule of 500, color + legend), column-matched filters, ranking. |
| 5   | Content quality (coverage)  | PARTIAL | Hero/meta imply full coverage, but only ~120/243 (~49%) lenses have full OQ scores; rest show "Limited professional review coverage." Handled transparently.    |

Every promise is delivered by a real, populated, working feature, content is
deep/sourced/current, and the product is fast and trustworthy — that is
A-territory. The single half-step deduction is finding #5: the headline "every
Fujifilm lens scored" outruns the actual ~49% OQ coverage. It is **not** a
broken feature (specs still render, no fabricated scores, "limited coverage"
messaging + beta badge keep it honest), so it does not pull below A-; but a bold
claim outpacing reality is exactly the kind of trust gap that keeps it from a
clean A. → tracked as #912.

the kind of thing that separates "sustainable" from "bulletproof."

## 4. Discovery (marketing perspective) — B

**Method:** evaluated findability and first impression from built titles/meta/OG,
headings, URL structure, sitemap, robots.txt — judging the _content_ of meta
tags, not their mere presence.

### Findings

| #   | Sub-dimension        | Status  | Finding                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| --- | -------------------- | ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Positioning          | PASS    | Clear what+who ("Every Fujifilm lens. Scored for your genre."); value prop stated; differentiated ("plenty of data, zero genre recommendations"); memorable name.                                                   |
| 2   | SEO effectiveness    | PASS    | Titles compelling + descriptive (26–50 chars); genre pages nail intent ("Best Lenses for Landscape Photography"); descriptions read as pitches; clean URLs. Minor: genre descriptions ~201 chars truncate in SERPs. |
| 3   | Social shareability  | PARTIAL | OG image exists + correct ratio, but it is a generic logo card reused on every page (no per-page data cards); no twitter:site/creator; social proof absent. → #914                                                  |
| 4   | Analytics & feedback | PASS    | Umami live on every page with real website-id; privacy-friendly, no consent banner; data collecting.                                                                                                                |
| 5   | Distribution & reach | PARTIAL | Fully indexable (static HTML, valid 460-URL sitemap, permissive robots, Product/Article/Org JSON-LD), but discovery beyond Google is thin: no community/social presence, no newsletter/RSS, only one real channel.  |

### Why B

The organic-search foundation is strong (sharp positioning, intent-matched
titles, clean indexable static pages with schema) — that part is A-grade. B
rather than higher because the two PARTIALs are about **amplification**, not
findability: a generic logo OG card instead of enticing per-page data previews
(#914), and a single discovery channel (Google) with no community seeding or
second independent channel. These cap reach, not quality — fixing the OG cards
and seeding Fuji communities would lift this to A.
