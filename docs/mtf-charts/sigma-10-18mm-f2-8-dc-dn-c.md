## Astigmatism assessment

S/M divergence at 30 lp/mm:

- Center to ~7.5mm: S and M track closely, gap < 0.03
- 10-12.5mm: moderate divergence, gap ~0.10-0.15
- 10 lp/mm lines stay tight until extreme edge where gap opens ~0.07

**Scoring:** Moderate divergence at edges → **1.5**

Per ADR-014 MTF chart fallback:

- S/M nearly overlapping → 2.0
- Moderate divergence → 1.0–1.5
- Heavy divergence → 0–0.5
