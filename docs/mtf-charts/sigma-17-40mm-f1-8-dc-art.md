## Astigmatism assessment

S/M divergence at 30 lp/mm:

- Center: slight gap ~0.04 (S at 0.94, M at 0.90)
- 7.5mm: gap widens to ~0.08
- 10-14mm: moderate divergence, gap ~0.07 at edge
- Both lines drop significantly at the edge but track together

**Scoring:** Moderate divergence → **1.5**

Note: LensTip lab review provides astigmatism: 2.0 (5.4%, measured). Lab data takes precedence.
