- Solid = Sagittal (S), Dashed = Meridional (M)
- Red lines = 10 lp/mm (contrast), Blue lines = 30 lp/mm (resolution)

## Astigmatism assessment

S/M divergence at 30 lp/mm:

- Center: gap already ~0.06 (S at 0.86, M at 0.80)
- Divergence increases across the frame
- At 10mm: gap ~0.07. At 14mm: gap recovers slightly but both drop significantly
- 10 lp/mm lines also show notable separation from ~5mm

**Scoring:** Heavy divergence starting early → **0.5**

Note: No lab astigmatism data exists for this lens. MTF chart is the only source.
