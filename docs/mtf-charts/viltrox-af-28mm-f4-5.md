## Astigmatism assessment

S/M divergence at 30 lp/mm:

- Maximum gap ~0.05 in the 6-10mm midframe zone
- S and M converge again at the edge
- 10 lp/mm lines nearly overlapping throughout

**Scoring:** S/M nearly overlapping across entire frame → **2.0**

Per ADR-014 MTF chart fallback:

- S/M nearly overlapping → 2.0
- Moderate divergence → 1.0–1.5
- Heavy divergence → 0–0.5
