## Astigmatism assessment

S/M divergence at 30 lp/mm:

- **At f/8:** S and M nearly overlapping across entire frame (<0.05 gap
  at all positions). Well corrected.
- **At f/1.4:** S/M closely matched at center, diverging from ~8mm outward.
  At 14mm edge: S ~0.30, M ~0.45 — gap of ~0.15. Heavy divergence.

**Scoring:** Heavy wide open, good stopped down → moderate overall → **1.0**

Per ADR-014 MTF chart fallback:

- S/M nearly overlapping → 2.0
- Moderate divergence → 1.0–1.5
- Heavy divergence → 0–0.5

Wide-open heavy + stopped-down good = **1.0** (conservative).
